How it works

This calculator uses the standard Elo rating systemto update player scores after a match. Beating a higher-rated opponent awards more points than beating a lower-rated one.

Dynamic K-factor

The K-factor controls how many points are at stake in a match. Newer or developing players move faster, while top ratings remain more stable:

  • 1000 – 2100: K = 32
  • 2100 – 2400: K = 24
  • Above 2400: K = 16

Rating Floor

All ratings have an absolute floor of 1000. A player at 1000 cannot lose additional points, though winners still earn their full rating gain.
